Rekordbox Export

Turn Serato crates into Rekordbox playlists, with hot cues, memory cues, saved loops and beatgrids. Read-only on your Serato library and music: the only thing written is the XML file you save.

How to use it

  1. Tick the crates to convert. Ticking a crate ticks the crates inside it.
  2. Preview shows every track with its cues, loops and BPM, plus anything that won't carry over. Nothing is saved.
  3. Export to Rekordbox saves the XML file.

Then in Rekordbox

  1. Settings > View > Layout: turn on rekordbox xml.
  2. Settings > Advanced > Database > rekordbox xml: Browse and choose the saved file.
  3. In the sidebar, open rekordbox xml > Playlists, right-click a playlist and choose Import Playlist.

Tracks already in your Rekordbox collection may keep their existing cues.
