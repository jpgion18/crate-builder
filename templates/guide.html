Quick Reference

The short version of every feature. Full step-by-step guides and the FAQ are on the Crate Builder website.

First time

  1. On Crate Builder, set your Music library folder and click Scan Library.
  2. Serato users: set the Serato folder to the one containing _Serato_ (on the external drive if your music lives there). Rekordbox-only DJs can leave it empty.
  3. For Spotify playlists, click Connect Spotify once.
  4. Back up your _Serato_ folder before your first build.

Build a crate from a list

Crate Builder tab

  1. Name the crate. Weddings > Smith puts it inside an existing crate.
  2. Paste a CSV, a Spotify playlist link, a public Dropbox/Google link, or Artist - Title lines. Click Preview Matches.
  3. Check the table. Press ▶ to listen, pick a version on ambiguous rows, use Find match for misses, and + Add another version for a second edit.
  4. Choose Build for (Serato, Rekordbox, or both) and click Build. In Serato, restart or rescan to see it.

Also on this tab: Missing Tracks Log (a CSV of songs you don't own) and Year/Genre Breakdown for a Spotify playlist.

Serato crates to Rekordbox and USB

Rekordbox tab

  1. Tick crates, click Preview to check cues and loops, then Export to Rekordbox and save the XML.
  2. In Rekordbox: Settings › View › Layout, turn on rekordbox xml.
  3. Settings › Advanced › Database › rekordbox xml: Browse to the saved file.
  4. In the sidebar, open rekordbox xml › Playlists, right-click a playlist and choose Import Playlist.
  5. Plug in your USB and drag the playlist onto it.

Find new music

Discover tab

Paste another DJ's tracklist and tag where it came from. Tracks you don't own go into the Discovery Log, and you can build a crate from selected entries anytime.

Clean up your library

Library Tools tab

Duplicate finder (review, pick the copy to keep, then remove the rest from Serato with a backup and undo), Year check against MusicBrainz, and a Metadata editor that backs up every file before saving.

Showfile (optional)

Settings, MyEvents, Community

Log in from Settings to pull couples' requests into MyEvents, sync crates back to events, and browse the Community feed.

Updates

Settings tab

A Download update button appears when a new version is out. Unzip it and replace the old app.

If something's off

  • A song didn't match: use Find match, or lower Match sensitivity a little.
  • The crate isn't in Serato: restart or rescan, and check the Serato folder is the one Serato uses.
  • Rekordbox can't read the XML: update Crate Builder and export again.
  • Cues are in the wrong place: tracks already in Rekordbox may keep their old cues. For MP3s, try MP3 cue shift.
  • Reporting a bug: click Copy report on the Rekordbox tab and send it along.
