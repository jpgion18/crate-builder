Before your first crate

Install and set up

  1. Download CrateBuilder-macos.zip or CrateBuilder-windows.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Open it the first time past the security warning. The app isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet.
    Mac: right-click CrateBuilder.app, choose Open, then Open again.
    Windows: on the blue SmartScreen box, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. On the main Crate Builder tab, set your Music library folder (it starts at your Music folder) and click Scan Library.
  4. Serato users: set the Serato folder to the one that contains _Serato_. If your music is on an external drive, it's usually on that drive. Rekordbox-only DJs can leave it empty.
  5. Want to paste Spotify playlists? Click Connect Spotify once and log in. No developer account or keys needed.

Back up your _Serato_ folder before your first build. Crate Builder only adds new crates and asks before replacing one with the same name, but a backup costs nothing.

By objective

What you can do

A

Turn a request list into a crate

For a couple's must-play list, a client's Spotify playlist, or a list someone texted you.

  1. Type a crate name. Use Weddings > Smith to put it inside an existing crate.
  2. Paste the list and click Preview Matches. It accepts a CSV, a Spotify playlist link, a public Dropbox or Google Drive/Docs/Sheets link, or plain lines like Artist - Title or Title by Artist.
  3. Check the table. Press ▶ to listen to a match. Rows marked ambiguous show the close versions (remixes, edits) so you pick one. Use Find match on anything it missed, and + Add another version to add two edits of one song.
  4. Pick Build for: Serato crate, Rekordbox playlist, or both. Click Build.
  5. Serato: restart it or rescan to see the crate. Rekordbox: save the XML and import it (see Serato to Rekordbox).

Click Missing Tracks Log to save a CSV of everything you don't own yet, as a shopping list.

Tab: Crate Builder
B

Move Serato crates to Rekordbox

Brings over hot cues, memory cues, saved loops and beatgrids, so you can play a Rekordbox setup or a club USB.

  1. Open the Rekordbox tab and tick the crates to convert. Ticking a crate also ticks the crates inside it.
  2. Click Preview to see every track's cues, loops and BPM, plus anything that won't carry over. Nothing is saved yet.
  3. Click Export to Rekordbox and save the XML file.
  4. In Rekordbox, go to Settings › View › Layout and turn on rekordbox xml.
  5. Go to Settings › Advanced › Database › rekordbox xml, click Browse, and choose the file.
  6. In the sidebar, open rekordbox xml › Playlists, right-click a playlist, and choose Import Playlist.
  7. For a USB stick, plug it in and drag the imported playlist onto it in Rekordbox, as you would with any playlist.

The ? button on the tab shows these steps too. Your Serato library and music files are only read, never changed.

Tab: Rekordbox
C

See what era and genre a playlist is

Paste a client's Spotify playlist and click Get Year/Genre Breakdown. You get a count per decade and per genre on screen, plus a CSV with every track. It's a quick way to see whether a couple's list leans 80s, 2000s hip-hop or current pop before you plan the rest of the night.

Tab: Crate Builder · needs Spotify connected
D

Find new music from other DJs' sets

Paste a tracklist from 1001tracklists, a Beatport chart, a Mixcloud or SoundCloud description, or a Spotify playlist, and label where it came from. Anything you don't already own goes into your Discovery Log, a running to-check list. Mark entries as acquired or dismissed, export it to CSV, or tick some and click Build a Crate from Selected.

Sources keeps bookmarks of where you dig, sorted by gig type: Wedding, Brunch, Happy Hour, Club Open and General.

Tab: Discover
E

Clean up your library

  • Duplicate finder reads Serato's own database and groups likely duplicates, with each file's path, year, key and pool. Tick the groups to clean up and pick the copy to keep: the others come out of Serato, crates switch to the kept copy, and the files move to a Removed Duplicates folder instead of being deleted. Quit Serato first. Every cleanup is backed up and can be undone.
  • Year check compares your year tags to MusicBrainz and flags mismatches. It checks about one track a second, so run big libraries in batches. It remembers where it stopped.
  • Metadata editor fixes title, artist, album, genre, year and track number. It backs up the file before every save, and Recent backups restores any version in one click.
Tab: Library Tools
F

Sync with Showfile (optional)

For DJs who run their gigs on Showfile. Log in from Settings and you get MyEvents, which pulls the songs couples add to their timelines and matches them in one click. You also get Sync to Showfile, which sends a finished crate back to an event, and Community, a feed of crate track lists shared by other DJs. Every other guide works without it.

Tabs: MyEvents, Community, Settings
