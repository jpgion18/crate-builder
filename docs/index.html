Crate Builder

Paste a request list, a Spotify playlist or a tracklist. Crate Builder finds those songs in your own music folder and builds a Serato crate, a Rekordbox playlist, or both. It also moves your Serato crates into Rekordbox with their cues and loops.

What it touches, and what it doesn't

Writes

  • New .crate files in your Serato Subcrates folder
  • XML and CSV files, only where you choose to save them
  • Your music files, only when you save in the Metadata editor (backed up first)

Never touches

  • Your existing crates or Serato's main database
  • Your Rekordbox library. You import the XML yourself.
  • Any file during Rekordbox export, duplicate finding or year checks

Back up your _Serato_ folder before your first build. Crate Builder only adds new crates and asks before replacing one with the same name, but a backup costs nothing.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Everything in the guides is free. The only extras are MyEvents, Sync to Showfile and Community, which need a Showfile account.

Does it upload my music or my library?

No. Matching and building happen on your computer. It only goes online to read a Spotify playlist or a shared Dropbox/Google link you paste in, to check MusicBrainz during a Year check, and once a day to see whether a new version is out.

Do I need Serato?

No. Rekordbox-only DJs can build Rekordbox playlists straight from a pasted list and leave the Serato folder empty. You need Serato only to build Serato crates, convert crates to Rekordbox, or use the Duplicate finder.

Do I need a Spotify account?

Only to paste Spotify playlist links or use the Year/Genre Breakdown. Click Connect Spotify and log in once. CSVs, shared links and plain track lists work without it.

My Mac or PC says the app is from an unknown developer

The app isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On a Mac, right-click CrateBuilder.app, choose Open, then Open again. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this the first time.

How do I update?

Settings shows a Download update button when a new version is out. Unzip the download and replace the old app. Your settings and logs are kept.

A song is in my library but it didn't match

Click Find match on that row and search by any word in the title or artist. If lots of songs miss, lower Match sensitivity a little. Messy file tags also hurt matching, and the Metadata editor fixes those.

My new crate doesn't show up in Serato

Restart Serato or rescan. Also check the Serato folder points at the _Serato_ folder Serato actually uses. With music on an external drive, that's often the one on the drive.

Rekordbox says it can't read the XML

Update to the newest Crate Builder from Settings and export again. Some older versions on Mac saved an error page instead of the playlist.

My cues are in the wrong place in Rekordbox

Songs already in your Rekordbox collection may keep their old cues. Some MP3s can also be a few milliseconds off between the two apps; the MP3 cue shift option adjusts for that. A track whose tempo changes partway through keeps only its first beatgrid marker, and the Preview report points these out.

What doesn't carry over to Rekordbox

Smart Crates aren't read. Cue colors come across but Rekordbox snaps them to its own palette. The Preview report lists anything else, such as missing files.

Crate Builder is a free app that runs on your own computer. Found a bug? Use Copy report on the Rekordbox tab, or describe what you did, and open an issue on GitHub.
